Five Bottlenecks I Found Inside Your Funnel - Amber Lyon

Install bottlenecks (#1-3 and #5) ship in less than 7 days from kickoff. Bottleneck #4 is an ongoing monthly engagement layer that compounds on top. Built as Install + Recurring Engagement.

Bottleneck #1 - Executive Coaching waitlist is unmonetised demand sitting on the page

The asymmetry: "AT CAPACITY · waitlist for May" means people are actively raising their hand to pay, and there is no productised path for them to convert today. Every name on that waitlist is either waiting for a 1-to-1 slot Amber doesn't have time to open, or quietly cooling because the only path is "wait until later."

A productised group DWY program at $5K-$10K (8-week cohort, methodology-led, 20-40 seats, run twice yearly) converts a meaningful share of the waitlist into paid cohort seats without Amber adding 1-to-1 hours. The seven Portal pillars (ROOT, FLOW, POWER, SELF, HEART, VOICE, VISION) are already the methodology footprint - the cohort is the implementation layer with live group calls, accountability, and Amber-led teaching, sitting above The Portal subscription.

Why it's costing her: - The waitlist exists because demand outstrips Amber's 1-to-1 capacity. Demand without a paid path is the textbook definition of money on the table. - 30-50% conversion of waitlist → paid cohort seat is realistic when the product is right-sized between Portal ($189/mo) and 1-to-1 (sold-out). - Each month the waitlist sits without a path, the warmest demand cools and the brand pays the cost of "AT CAPACITY" without collecting the upside.

The fix: - "Magnetic Method Cohort" (working title) - 8-week productised DWY program - Built around the seven Portal pillars · 1 pillar per week with the 8th week as integration - Live group calls weekly with Amber + private cohort chat + tailored worksheets - 20-40 seats per cohort, capped intentionally · run twice yearly (Spring + Autumn intakes) - $5K-$10K per seat · positioned as the cohort layer between The Portal and 1-to-1 - Auto-routes existing Executive Coaching waitlist as priority access

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $225K-$450K incremental at 30-60 buyers x $7,500 average. Recurring twice yearly. Compounds because cohort graduates feed both the Portal renewal base and a future Premium 1-to-1 selection pool.

Why this is #1: Highest ceiling. Most asymmetric. Pre-validated by the existing waitlist signal. Everything downstream is easier when this layer exists.

Bottleneck #2 - Linktree is a 9-link buffet routing 269K to indecision

The leak: The 269K @modernmind___ audience clicks the bio link and lands on a Linktree with 9+ options: Portal, book, app, podcast, 1-to-1, Reset, social, etc. No hierarchy, no segmentation, no ONE primary CTA above the fold. The conversion math at a Linktree buffet is brutal: most clicks scroll, scan, and bounce. The link click → email subscriber → buyer rate is a fraction of what a custom one-page bio site would deliver at the same audience size.

A custom bio site at modernmind.earth/start (or /go) replaces the Linktree with ONE primary CTA above the fold (Magnetic Archetype quiz → email capture → segmented routing). Below the fold, the same 9 destinations live as scannable secondary cards. The quiz is the new primary entry point; everything else is supporting.

Why it's costing her: - Linktree-style multi-link buffets convert at 1-3% to a paid action. Custom one-page bio sites with a single primary CTA convert at 8-15% to email and 1-2% to a paid action on first session. At 269K IG audience with even modest weekly link click traffic, the gap is meaningful. - No quiz = no segmentation. Amber currently can't tell a Portal-ready buyer from a book-curious browser from a 1-to-1 enquirer at the moment they enter the funnel. - Email list quality decays without segmentation. Broadcast emails to a non-segmented list under-perform compared to segmented sends.

The fix: - Custom bio site at modernmind.earth/start (or /go) - one-page, mobile-first, brand-matched (purple #8f5e93 over soft cream) - Primary CTA above the fold: "Magnetic Archetype quiz · 90 seconds · Find which Portal pillar to lead with" - Quiz returns 1 of 4-5 archetype results, captures email, segments by primary pillar interest - Below the fold: the existing 9 destinations as ranked cards (Portal first, book second, podcast/app third, 1-to-1/Reset/social trailing) - Update Instagram bio link from Linktree to modernmind.earth/start

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $40K-$120K recovered conversion - book sales lifted, Portal email sign-ups doubled, cohort waitlist captured at point of entry. Compounds across every IG post that points to the bio link.

Why this is #2: Foundational. Bottleneck #1 (cohort) needs a healthy email pipe. Bottleneck #3 (book→Portal) needs a quiz to segment book-curious traffic. The bio funnel is the connective tissue. Fast install, high leverage.

Bottleneck #3 - "You Are a Magnet" book buyers route nowhere

The contradiction: The book launched June 2024. ~11 months in market with NYT-author Yung Pueblo's endorsement and a still-active pre-order page. Book buyers are the warmest possible segment in the entire Modern Mind ecosystem. They've already paid for Amber's voice and methodology. They've sat with the magnetism metaphor for 200+ pages. And right now, after the Amazon checkout completes, they go nowhere.

A "free chapter + Magnetic Archetype quiz" funnel captures book-curious traffic before purchase, then nurtures buyers post-purchase into The Portal ($189/mo) using an 8-touch email sequence built around the chapters they just read. Every chapter has a corresponding pillar in the Portal. Every reader who finished the book is a Portal subscription waiting to be invited.

Why it's costing her: - 3-7% of book buyers will convert to a $189/mo membership when the offer is positioned right. At even modest book volume, that compounds into recurring revenue the funnel never collects today. - Book buyers are the cheapest customer to convert (already paid, already engaged with the methodology). Letting them fall off after Amazon checkout is the opposite of compound funnel design. - The book's continued sales (still on the pre-order page 11 months in) means this is a leak that keeps leaking - every new reader is another non-converted Portal subscriber unless this funnel exists.

The fix: - Pre-purchase: "Read chapter one free + take the Magnetic Archetype quiz" landing page · captures email before book purchase - Post-purchase: 8-touch nurture sequence over 30 days, one touch per book chapter / Portal pillar, ending with a Portal trial offer - Includes one Yung Pueblo quote callback in the sequence (one only · respect the co-sign) - Trigger: book purchase confirmation OR quiz completion

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $50K-$150K recurring (compounds with book volume). Recurring because Portal is monthly subscription, so each converted reader produces ~$2,300 LTV at average tenure.

Why this is #3: Smallest immediate ceiling but the highest compound coefficient because it's recurring. Dependent on Bottleneck #2 (the quiz lives at the bio funnel and on the book landing page). Sits last in install order because it leans on the infrastructure built by #1 and #2.

Bottleneck #5 (Install) - Book → Portal Nurture Funnel Is The Lowest-Friction Install Lever

Snapshot. "You Are a Magnet" launched June 2024. The book is in 5+ languages, available on Amazon, endorsed by Yung Pueblo (NYT bestselling author). Book buyers are the warmest audience segment Amber has, they've already invested 4+ hours with her voice. And there's no observable post-purchase nurture funnel routing them into The Portal ($1,590 lifetime / $189/mo membership) or the Executive Coaching waitlist.

Asymmetry. Industry norm for author-coach stacks is a free-chapter / archetype quiz funnel that captures book-buyer emails and runs an 8-14 day chapter-mapped nurture sequence routing readers into the membership tier. Conversion 2-5% of book buyers to recurring subscription at Amber's audience density is conservative. The book is in 5+ languages. The IP is set. The endorsement is locked. The only missing layer is the email capture and the sequence.

Why it's costing her. Every book sale that doesn't capture the email is a permanent loss of warm-audience inventory. June 2024 to now is ~23 months of book sales walking past the funnel. Each new reader who finishes the book without a nurture path is a Portal subscription that never gets invited and a cohort applicant that never gets surfaced.

The fix. Free chapter download landing + Magnetic Archetype quiz + 8-touch chapter-mapped email sequence → tier-routed CTA (Reset for early-stage readers, Portal for ready-to-commit, Executive Coaching cohort for advanced).

Estimated impact. $40K-$160K Year-1 floor on the nurture install. At her stage (5+ languages, Yung Pueblo foreword, sustained Amazon traffic), the only piece missing is the email infrastructure to convert warm book-buyer audience into paid program enrolment.

Why this is #5 (and the lowest-friction Install lift). Cohort funnel (#1) requires productising the methodology and filling a fresh enrolment window. Bio funnel (#2) requires replacing the live Linktree. Book → Portal recurring (#3) requires the quiz infrastructure from #2 to land first. The free-chapter + archetype-quiz nurture (#5) is the install with the least new content production required - the book is written, the IP is set, the endorsement is locked. Only the email infrastructure and landing page need building. Highest-leverage, lowest-friction Install lever in the package.

Bottleneck #4 - Distribution-side: audience-yes, X / LinkedIn / YouTube-no

Snapshot. 269K IG @modernmind + 7,000+ Substack subscribers + Modern Mind Podcast + "You Are a Magnet" in 5+ languages = audience-yes. The IP, the resonance, the methodology footprint are all proven. The distribution side tells a different story: X @modernmind exists at the same handle as IG but engagement and cadence sit dramatically below the IG profile at her audience tier. LinkedIn personal presence is zero - the "Modern Mind" company page that surfaces in search has 4 followers and unrelated staff, and the salon-stylist Amber Lyon in search results is a different person. YouTube long-form home base is absent at her audience tier - the visible YouTube footprint is guest appearances on other shows (Millennial Mind Podcast #151, Okay Sister, How Do You Do It) generating views for hosts who own the long-form home base, not for Modern Mind.

Asymmetry. AG1's actual category positioning is operator-tier ghostwriting. The comparable AG1 case study: a ghostwriting client cracked 250K views/week in the first week starting from under 6,000 followers. That's the asymmetric play - someone else writes and ships at the operator's voice from existing IP while the operator stays in their highest-leverage work. Amber already has more raw IP than the comparable - the book gives 200+ pages of methodology, the Substack archive proves the long-form voice, the Modern Mind Podcast catalogue gives the audio source, and the seven Portal pillars give the structural anchor. The ghostwriting layer doesn't invent the voice; it scales it across the channels she currently doesn't touch.

Why it's costing her. - X is leaking ~$30K-$80K/year in Portal subs + book sales. Growing X from current (~10-30K, visibly below the IG profile) to 100K+ in 12 months is realistic at her IP density. 100K X followers at her Portal funnel conversion ratio = $30K-$80K/year incremental. - LinkedIn personal is the missing channel for the Executive Coaching offer - and the largest single dollar opportunity. Building from ~0 to 30K+ personal followers in 12 months qualifies her for corporate wellness and executive-coaching B2B leads. Each B2B exec-coaching client at $20K-$50K/year. 4-8 corporate clients/year realistic = $100K-$400K incremental Year-1 from a channel she currently doesn't touch. - YouTube long-form is leaking $47K-$95K/year in recurring Portal subs. With her IP and cross-pollination from IG, realistic to grow to 50K subs in 12 months. The asymmetric play isn't ad revenue (small at wellness CPMs) - it's Portal subscription conversion from YouTube long-form viewers. 0.5-1% conversion at 50K subs = 250-500 Portal subs at $189/mo lowest tier = $47K-$95K/year recurring (8.5x that if subscribers pick $1,590 lifetime instead).

The fix. Monthly ghostwriting engagement: AG1 writes and ships at her voice from her existing IP. Three channels, one editorial cadence. - X cadence: 3x/day - threads, replies, ecosystem engagement. Source material: Portal pillar essays, Substack archive, podcast catalogue, book chapters. - LinkedIn cadence: 4-5x/week long-form posts positioning the Executive Coaching offer for C-suite + corporate wellness buyers. Source material: same IP, B2B-translated. - YouTube long-form cadence: 1-2 videos/week. No filming required - script + thumbnail strategy, recorded once per week by her team or via voiceover. Source material: book chapters and Portal pillars rewritten as 8-15 minute educational long-form.

Estimated impact. Year-1 floor on the recurring engagement phase ALONE: $150K-$500K+. Combined with the Install phase floor of $355K-$880K (bottlenecks #1, #2, #3, #5), total combined Year-1 ceiling is $505K-$1.38M+.

Why this is #4 (and the highest-recurring play). Install bottlenecks (#1-3) are one-shot revenue lifts - they build the funnel surfaces, the cohort runs, the recurring revenue starts. The distribution layer is the ONLY recurring asset that compounds month-on-month - every month the audience grows and feeds back into the Install layers as more cohort applicants, more book sales, more Portal subs. The order matters: Install first (week 1) so the surfaces exist to capture the traffic. The recurring engagement kicks in week 2+ as the distribution engine starts pointing audiences at the Install-launched product surfaces.
