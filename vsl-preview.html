VSL Preview Script - Amber Lyon

Phase 1 deliverable · Short preview script structure, page layout, production notes for the Magnetic Method Cohort hero VSL.

Format note: This is the short preview version (3-5 min) for the cohort landing page. Full long-form VSL is a Phase 2 enhancement once the cohort has run twice and we have testimonial footage worth lengthening into.

Format

3-5 minute hero VSL hosted on Wistia, embedded in the landing-page hero. Soft-feminine register matching Amber's book voice. Single take where possible, supplemented by light B-roll (notebook, candle, hardcover book on desk, soft window light). No music under the talking head. Light ambient bed under B-roll only.

Cold Open (00:00 - 00:30)

On camera, Amber, soft window light:

"If you finished You Are a Magnet and felt something settle, this is the conversation that comes after the book. The Magnetic Method Cohort is the version of the methodology where the seven pillars get installed in real time, with a group of women doing the same work in the same room. Eight weeks. One pillar at a time. Live with me each week."

What The Cohort Is (00:30 - 01:30)

The cohort is the bridge between the book and the deeper work. Eight weeks. Seven pillars (ROOT, FLOW, POWER, SELF, HEART, VOICE, VISION) plus an integration week. Live group calls each week with Amber. A private cohort chat. Tailored reflection worksheets.

This isn't a course. The Portal is the course. The cohort is the live container - the version where the work happens with a group around you, not alone in a feed.

What The Cohort Is Not (01:30 - 02:00)

Not a manifestation course. Not a vision-board weekend. Not the same content as the Portal repackaged. Not for the woman who hasn't read the book yet (start there).

This is for the woman who already knows the methodology and wants to move it from the page into the body, with a group of women doing the same work alongside her.

Who I Am (02:00 - 02:45)

Quick-hit context, not a chronology:

  • Author of "You Are a Magnet" (June 2024, foreword reference Yung Pueblo)
  • Founder of Modern Mind, the Portal membership, and Self FM / RADIATE meditation app
  • Coach, eight years of one-to-one work with women rebuilding self-worth and nervous-system capacity
  • Based in New Zealand · cohort runs across NZ + UK + US time-friendly windows

The point isn't the credentials. The point is the work behind them.

What Eight Weeks Looks Like (02:45 - 03:45)

  • Week 1 - ROOT - the body before the thought
  • Week 2 - FLOW - the nervous-system rhythm that runs your week
  • Week 3 - POWER - the difference between assertion and over-functioning
  • Week 4 - SELF - self-trust as the asset most women under-build
  • Week 5 - HEART - the receiver layer
  • Week 6 - VOICE - what gets said and what doesn't
  • Week 7 - VISION - the future self the body is already aware of
  • Week 8 - integration - the pillars together, in the room with each other

Each week: 75-minute live call with me, a reflection worksheet, and a private cohort chat that stays open.

Who This Is For - And Who It Isn't (03:45 - 04:15)

For: women who have read the book or are deep in the work and want the group teaching layer. Women who want a cohort, not a self-paced membership. Women ready for a 6-8 hour weekly commitment for eight weeks.

Not for: women who haven't read the book or done foundational nervous-system work (start with the Portal first). Women looking for a 1-to-1 (the cohort waitlist routes the few 1-to-1 spots that open). Women looking for a quick fix.

Investment + What's Included (04:15 - 04:45)

The cohort sits between the Portal subscription and 1-to-1 access. Hint pricing on the page. Includes 8 live calls with Amber, the cohort workbook, the private cohort chat for the duration, and integration week.

Close (04:45 - 05:00)

"If the book named the principles, this is where they get installed. The application form is below the video. Two intakes a year. - Amber."

Production Notes

  • Filming location: Amber's home studio (talking head) · single window-light setup
  • Camera: main camera + B-camera + lavalier mic. Standard-issue Sony A7C or equivalent + Røde Wireless Pro
  • Wardrobe: soft cream / oat / matte black palette. No logos. Minimal jewellery
  • Lighting: natural window light, soft. No hard key lights
  • Pace: target 1.0x watchable. Soft pauses are fine, breath is part of the register. No quick cuts
  • B-roll: notebook open on desk, candle, "You Are a Magnet" hardcover, hands writing, view through the window. Editorial pacing
  • Music: none under talking head. Light ambient bed under B-roll only. Royalty-free, library-grade
  • Captions: burnt-in for IG / YT shorts. SRT for long-form Wistia
  • Thumbnail (Wistia poster): Amber at desk, soft window light, book in frame, looking off-camera. Title overlay: "Where the book becomes the work."
