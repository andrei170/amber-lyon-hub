Every name on that waitlist is either waiting for a 1-to-1 slot you don't have time to open, or quietly cooling because the only path is "wait until later." A productised group DWY program at $5K-$10K (8-week cohort, 20-40 seats, run twice yearly) converts the waitlist into paid cohort seats without you adding 1-to-1 hours. The seven Portal pillars (ROOT, FLOW, POWER, SELF, HEART, VOICE, VISION) are already the methodology footprint - the cohort is the implementation layer.

~$19-37K/mo in cohort revenue not collected while the waitlist sits on the page. That compounds to ~$225-450K/yr in unbooked seats while the warm demand cools. Each month the waitlist stays without a path, the women who raised their hand drift to other coaches who have a productised group tier ready.

6 months from now ·~$112-225K never collected. Plus the next wave of 1-to-1 enquirers - same waitlist, same dead-end, same cooling demand.

Linktree Is A 9-Link Buffet Routing 269K To Indecision

The 269K @modernmind___ audience clicks the bio link and lands on a 9-option Linktree with no hierarchy. Most clicks scroll, scan, and bounce. The link click → email subscriber rate is a fraction of what a custom one-page bio site would deliver at the same audience size. A custom bio site at modernmind.earth/start with ONE primary CTA above the fold (Magnetic Archetype quiz → email capture → segmented routing) lifts IG-bio-click → email-subscriber by 2x to 4x. Below the fold, the same 9 destinations live as scannable secondary cards.

Cost of Waiting

~$3-10K/mo in book sales + Portal sign-ups + cohort waitlist captures not happening - ~$40-120K/yr compounding for life of the bio funnel you don't have. Without a quiz, the audience can't be segmented at point of entry. Without segmentation, every broadcast underperforms.

6 months from now ·~$20-60K in conversion never recovered. Plus a non-segmented email list that keeps under-converting on every send.

Fix · One-page bio site at modernmind.earth/start. Magnetic Archetype quiz (Typeform, 90 sec, 5 archetypes). Email capture + archetype segmentation. Year-1 floor $40-120K recovered conversion, compounds for life of the bio funnel.

"You Are A Magnet" Book Buyers Route Nowhere

The book launched June 2024. ~11 months in market with Yung Pueblo's foreword endorsement. Book buyers are the warmest possible segment in the Modern Mind ecosystem. They've already paid for your voice and methodology. They've sat with the magnetism metaphor for 200+ pages. And after the Amazon checkout completes, they go nowhere. A free-chapter + Magnetic Archetype quiz funnel captures book-curious traffic, then a 30-day chapter-mapped sequence nurtures buyers into The Portal subscription. Every chapter has a corresponding pillar. Every reader who finished the book is a Portal subscription waiting to be invited.

Cost of Waiting

~$4-12K/mo in recurring Portal revenue never collected from book buyers. ~$50-150K/yr compounding because Portal is a monthly subscription. Each new reader who finishes the book without a nurture path is recurring revenue the funnel never collects.

6 months from now ·~$25-75K in subscription LTV never started. Plus a backlog of warm readers who could have been Portal subscribers and never got the invitation.

Fix · "Free chapter + Magnetic Archetype quiz" pre-purchase landing + 30-day chapter-mapped post-purchase sequence into a Portal trial offer. Year-1 floor $50-150K recurring (compounds with book volume).
