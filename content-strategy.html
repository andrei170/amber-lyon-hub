Podcast Plan

Current state: Modern Mind Podcast is live (Apple ID 1719364801, Spotify). Cadence and episode structure show through public listings. No visible CTA back into the Portal or quiz on the episode listing pages.

Phase 3 move: Add a 30-second standardised CTA segment at the end of each episode pointing listeners to modernmind.earth/start for the Magnetic Archetype quiz. Pin a 4-episode "Magnetic Method Foundations" series at the top of the feed, each episode mapping to a Portal pillar (ROOT, FLOW, POWER, SELF). The remaining three pillars (HEART, VOICE, VISION) become the next series, recorded in batch.

Why: zero new content infrastructure, surgical CTA addition, leverages existing podcast IP, routes listeners into the quiz funnel.

Photoshoot Brief

Two-day shoot, mid-cycle in Phase 3 (Week 7-8 ideal). NZ-based, Amber's home studio + outdoor natural-light location.

Day 1 - Studio · Soft Indoor Setup: Amber at desk, notebook, candle, neutral palette, "You Are a Magnet" hardcover in frame, soft window light. Lavender / cream / matte black palette. 200+ stills, 3-4 hours of B-roll for talking-head IG cutdowns.

Day 2 - Outdoor · Natural Setting: Amber walking, pausing, breath-and-stillness shots. Natural NZ landscape (beach or quiet forest). Editorial pacing. 150+ stills, 2-3 hours of B-roll for episode trailers and short-form.

Outputs feed: cohort landing page, Portal subscription page, Magnetic Archetype quiz result page, 40-hook IG content calendar, ad creative library when Phase 4 paid traffic activates.

Short-Form Roadmap (90 days)

  • Weeks 4-6: post 5 hooks/week from Categories 1-3 (subconscious, self-worth, nervous system) - the highest-resonance segments for the IG audience
  • Weeks 7-9: post 5/week from Categories 4-6 (magnetism, Portal pillars, book bridge) · book + Portal cross-pollination
  • Weeks 10-12: post 5/week from Categories 7-8 (founder voice, behind-the-book) · authority + intimacy layer that pulls cohort applicants

Cadence: 5 IG reels + 3 YT shorts + 1 long-form podcast / week. Repurpose ratio 1:5 - one filmed studio asset = one long-form podcast episode + 5 short-form cuts.

The bio link points to modernmind.earth/start (replacing the current Linktree). Once the quiz is live, every IG post compounds toward a tracked email sign-up with archetype segmentation.
