Sequence 1 · Magnetic Archetype Welcome Flow (10 days, 8 touches)

Triggered when a new subscriber finishes the Magnetic Archetype quiz at modernmind.earth/start. Segmented by archetype result (5 archetypes) and primary Portal pillar interest.

T+0 - Immediate (Archetype Result)

Subject: Your Magnetic Archetype is [name] - here is what that means Body: Archetype reveal, 1 paragraph context on what this archetype tends to under-build (self-trust, capacity, voice, or vision depending on result), 3-bullet pattern map, link to download a printable archetype reflection PDF. Soft mention of "You Are a Magnet" as the place to read more. Goal: deliver promise, set expectations, position the methodology.

T+1 day - The Bridge

Subject: The chapter most readers re-read three times Body: A specific story or reflection on the Portal pillar that maps to her archetype. Closes with "the Magnetic Method Cohort is the version of the methodology where this gets installed in real time." Goal: bridge book reader / quiz-taker into the cohort frame.

T+3 days - The Pattern Story

Subject: The pattern your nervous system runs on Sunday night Body: Pattern-led teaching, body-aware register. Anchored to the archetype the reader received. CTA: take the next pillar reflection at modernmind.earth. Goal: prove the method depth, build trust through specificity.

T+5 days - The Self-Worth Story

Subject: The receipt of self-worth lives somewhere unexpected Body: Vulnerable, considered, founder-voice paragraph from Amber. The morning Modern Mind almost shut down · the boundary that rebuilt it. CTA → cohort waitlist. Goal: build trust through admitted uncertainty.

T+7 days - The Book Bridge

Subject: The chapter you skipped (and why it pays) Body: Quote from a chapter most readers skim in the book. Why it's load-bearing. The cohort installs it. CTA → cohort. Goal: route book readers and quiz-takers into the cohort buying frame.

T+9 days - Soft Pitch Open

Subject: A quiet question before I close the inbox Body: Two-line check-in. "Is the Magnetic Method Cohort a fit for where you're at, or is the Portal the next step?" Link to cohort. "Or hit reply and tell me which pillar feels most off-axis." Goal: segment hot vs warm; route hot to cohort, warm to a 1-to-1 reply for triage.

T+10 days - Last Touch (segmented)

  • Archetype A (cohort-fit): Last call for the next cohort intake. CTA → cohort apply page.
  • Archetype B (Portal-fit): Portal trial offer at $189/mo or $1,590 lifetime. CTA → Portal.
  • Archetype C/D/E (foundations / book / podcast pool): "Start with the book and the podcast. Then we talk." → "You Are a Magnet" link + Modern Mind Podcast subscribe.

Sequence 2 · Book Buyer 30-Day Chapter-Mapped Sequence (8 touches)

Triggered by "You Are a Magnet" purchase confirmation OR free-chapter quiz completion. One touch every 3-4 days, mapped to the chapters as the reader moves through the book.

T+1 day - Welcome Reader

Subject: Welcome - here is the way to read the first chapter Body: A 1-paragraph note on how to pace the book. The first chapter is the threshold. Reflection prompt. Soft note that the Portal extends each chapter into a pillar. Goal: make the reader feel met, set the tempo.

T+4 days - Chapter 1-2 Reflection

Subject: What you noticed in the first two chapters Body: Reader reflection prompts mapped to chapter 1 + 2 themes. Soft Portal pillar tie-in (ROOT). No CTA push, just deepening. Goal: stay close to the book voice, not pull away from it.

T+7 days - Chapter 3-4 Reflection

Subject: The pattern under the chapter Body: Specific journal prompt for the reader. Pillar tie-in (FLOW). First soft mention of The Portal as the place where pillar work lives. Goal: introduce the Portal without selling it.

T+10 days - Yung Pueblo Co-Sign Callback

Subject: What Yung Pueblo wrote in the foreword Body: One specific reference to the Yung Pueblo endorsement. What it meant to Amber. Ties back to the chapter the reader is in. CTA: Magnetic Archetype quiz at modernmind.earth/start. Goal: credibility callback (one only - respect the co-sign).

T+14 days - Mid-Book Pause

Subject: A pause around the middle of the book Body: Most readers slow down here. That is the work. Pillar tie-in (POWER + SELF). Portal link as the place to keep moving when the book asks you to sit. Goal: soft Portal positioning at the natural pause point.

T+18 days - Pillar Deep-Dive

Subject: The pillar most readers under-build Body: A teaching paragraph on whichever pillar matches the reader's archetype (if quiz was taken) or HEART by default. Portal trial CTA. Goal: route to Portal trial.

T+24 days - Reader Story

Subject: A reader I keep writing for Body: A composite reader story (no PII, anonymised) - the woman who read the book in one weekend and joined the Portal six weeks later. CTA → Portal. Goal: social proof through narrative without naming individuals.

T+30 days - The Portal Trial

Subject: Where the book becomes the work Body: Direct, soft Portal trial offer. $189/mo or $1,590 lifetime. The cohort mentioned at the bottom for the reader who wants the group teaching layer. Two CTAs, Portal first, cohort waitlist second. Goal: clean Portal trial pitch at the end of the sequence; no pressure, clean exit if not a fit.

Sequence 3 · Cohort Pre-Call Nurture (3 touches before discovery call)

Triggered when a Magnetic Method Cohort applicant books a 1-to-1 enrolment call (optional add-on, not all cohort buyers need a call).

T-7 days

Subject: Before our call - 3 reflections to bring Body: Three reflection prompts to sit with before the call. Helps the applicant arrive prepared.

T-3 days

Subject: What I learned working with [N] women last cohort Body: Story-driven. One specific "before-call" insight from the prior cohort. Sets the room.

T-1 day

Subject: Tomorrow at [time] - link inside, one thing to bring Body: Calendar reminder, one specific prep ask (the pillar she most wants the cohort to anchor on, plus the pattern she most wants to work).

Sequence 4 · Cohort Post-Call Follow-Up (4 touches over 14 days)

Triggered after the discovery call regardless of outcome.

T+1 hour - Recap + Next Steps

Subject: Recap from our call + 2 next steps Body: 1-paragraph recap, 2 specific action items Amber committed to, link to the agreed-upon path (cohort enrolment / Portal trial / wait list for next intake).

T+3 days - The Specific Resource

Subject: That [reflection / pillar exercise] I mentioned on the call Body: Whatever specific resource Amber promised on the call (a specific pillar exercise, a meditation from Self FM RADIATE, a chapter quote from the book). Sent in clean PDF or shared Notion.

T+7 days - The Check-In

Subject: Quick - did you sit with [X]? Body: Soft accountability nudge. "Reply with a one-line update if you did. Reply with a question if you didn't."

T+14 days - The Decision

Subject: Where you landed Body: Clean ask: "Are we moving forward with [agreed path] or holding for the next cohort?" No pressure. Clear decision frame. Either route triggers the right next sequence (cohort onboarding or long-term Portal nurture).
