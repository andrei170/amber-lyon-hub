Foundation

Copy Strategy - Amber Lyon

Foundation doc · Voice, brand pillars, headline bank, framing AG1 will write to.

Voice

Feminine, soft, magnetic, methodology-led. Amber's writing register is warm, considered, body-aware, and never hyped. The book voice ("You Are a Magnet") is the calibration target - principles delivered in plain prose, the metaphor of magnetism running quietly underneath. Anti-guru, pro-method. Talks to the reader as a thinker, never down to her.

Use: magnetic, magnetism, alignment, self-worth, self-trust, nervous system, regulation, capacity, subconscious, pattern, frequency, the body, the work, soften, hold, return, the room, the woman, the reader, the threshold Avoid: game-changer, revolutionary, unlock your potential, level up, hustle, grind, alpha, transform your life, next-level, manifest your dreams (avoid the cliche even in a manifestation niche), elevate your, paradigm shift, harness the power, seamlessly, effortlessly

Brand Pillars

  1. Magnetism as method - the brand metaphor and the working framework. Magnetism is a frequency the nervous system runs, not a personality trait you summon.
  2. Subconscious-first - the work changes the pattern under the thought, not the thought on top of it.
  3. Capacity over calm - regulation isn't a quiet life. It's a wider window for what life sends.
  4. The reader as a thinker - the audience is treated as women already doing the work. The copy meets them as peers, not as students who need rescuing.

Headline Bank

For the Magnetic Method Cohort landing page, hub callouts, and ad creative when paid traffic activates:

  • The cohort version of the methodology.
  • Eight weeks. Seven pillars. One nervous system, regulating in real time.
  • For the woman on the May waitlist - and the one who already finished the book.
  • The Magnetic Method, taught the way it was meant to be taught.
  • Where the book becomes the work.
  • Eight weeks of group teaching, one pillar at a time.
  • You read the book. This is what comes after.
  • The cohort the waitlist was waiting for.

Framing for the Funnel

  • Top of funnel (quiz, IG hooks, podcast cold opens): pattern-led and body-aware, not promise-led. "The pattern your nervous system runs every Sunday night" beats "manifest your dream life" by every metric that matters.
  • Mid funnel (cohort landing, VSL, book → Portal nurture): "You Are a Magnet" as the bridge. The book named the principles; the cohort installs them.
  • High funnel (1-to-1 / continuation): for the cohort graduate who wants depth - the room above the room. Sold by reference, not by page.

Banned Phrases

Universal AG1 ban list, plus Amber-specific: - No "manifest your dreams" (cliche even inside the niche) - No "high-vibe" / "low-vibe" (overused in the wellness lane) - No "boss babe" / "queen energy" / "divine feminine" framing (off-register) - No "transform" (unless paired with a concrete pattern shift) - No "elevate your" (her brand uses adjacent phrasing - we hold the line) - No first-month-lift language without the NDA-aware "$0 to $172k/mo first month" framing only

Tone Calibration By Surface

Surface Register Length
IG hook Pattern-led, body-aware, single-line 1 line
Ad copy (when live) Promise + mechanism, soft register 2-3 lines
Email subject Question or specific image, never clickbait 5-9 words
VSL opening Quiet, considered, story-led cold open 2-3 sentence cold open
Landing hero What's at stake for the woman who finished the book 1 promise + 1 mechanism
FAQ Plain answers, no hedge, peer-to-peer 2-3 sentences each

Voice Test

Every paragraph passes if a reader who finished "You Are a Magnet" in one weekend nods, not flinches. If it could appear in a generic "manifestation course" sales page, rewrite it. If it would feel out of place in the book, it's out of place on the cohort page.
